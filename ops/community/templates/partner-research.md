# Partner research checklist

Do not scrape directories. Do not fill in a contact until a person has verified the organization, the role, and the email from a page or a reply.

Look for organizations that already gather Utah small-business owners:

- Chambers of commerce
- Public libraries with business programs
- Coworking spaces
- SBDC and SCORE-type advising groups
- Trade and professional associations

For each candidate, record only what you verified:

1. Organization name and city.
2. The public page that describes their events or membership.
3. Whether they host outside speakers.
4. A named contact only after the name and role are on that page or in a reply.
5. The date you checked.

Put verified rows in `partner-contacts.csv`. Leave the file empty until a row is verified.
