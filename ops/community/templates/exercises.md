# Workshop exercises

Founder review required before these exercises are handed out.

Pick exercises that match the host brand.

## Fripse AI

- Rewrite a real customer email with ChatGPT, then mark what a person still needs to check.
- List three repetitive tasks from the last week and circle one that might be reduced with a tool the business already has.
- Draft a short team rule for what information should not be pasted into a chatbot.

## FoundForAI

- Read one page of the business website the way an AI assistant would: what is the business, who is it for, and what is missing?
- Find whether the site states its city and services in plain language.
- List one change the owner can make without a new vendor.

Do not score attendees or promise a ranking.
