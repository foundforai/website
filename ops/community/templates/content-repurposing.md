## Rules

- FoundForAI: AI search visibility, agent-ready websites, MCP and WebMCP, AI integrations.
- Fripse AI: ChatGPT in daily work, assessments, workflow automation, employee training, roadmaps, support.
- One host brand if the piece is about an event.
- No invented metrics, quotes, or partner claims.
- No em dashes.
- Do not mention OpenWorks on a public page.
- Founder review required before publishing.
